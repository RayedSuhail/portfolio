About

Who I am

I am a Systems Analyst at Equitable Financial and a researcher specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity. With a Master's research background supported by the National Science Foundation (NSF) at Purdue University Northwest, my academic work focuses on developing lightweight CNN architectures for real-time Wi-Fi intrusion detection and creating educational frameworks for adversarial and privacy-preserving machine learning. I bridge the gap between theoretical AI research and practical enterprise application -- building resilient, trustworthy digital systems designed for real-world reliability.
